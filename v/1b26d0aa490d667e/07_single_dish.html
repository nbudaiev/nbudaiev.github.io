1

Why combine arrays

Each array measures a different range of spacings. Structure larger than the 12-m MRS needs the 7-m array and Total Power.

Fixed by the observation12-m · 7-m · Total Power

ALMA is 3 arrays in 1

TryAdd the 7-m array, then Total Power. Each fills part of the central hole left by the one before. Before combining, compare the arrays on the overlapping spacings (shaded): amplitudes, weights, rms.

12-m array

Up to 50 × 12-m antennas, baselines 15 m to 16 km.

7-m array (ACA)

12 × 7-m antennas, baselines ≈9–45 m.

Total Power (TP, ACA)

4 × 12-m single dishes. Autocorrelation spectra, on-the-fly maps.

Fixed by the observationmissing short spacings · flux recovery

The short-spacing problem

TryGrow the cloud with the 12-m array only. Near the MRS its flux is lost and a shallow negative bowl appears (Deepest negative). Larger clouds vanish. The compact sources are kept. Add the 7-m array, then TP (feather). The cloud peak is fixed, so a bigger cloud has more total flux.

Primary beam ignored (uniform sensitivity). The extra short spacings of a mosaic are not simulated.

Preparing the TP image for feather

TrySet expr='IM0' (TP alone). The TP part is then not attenuated by the pb, and impbcor raises it by 1/pb toward the edge. Both cases are less accurate near the edge, so trim to the inner region.

  1. imhead: the TP and interferometer cubes must have the same restfreq. Pipeline TP cubes may not.
  2. imregrid the TP cube onto the 12-m + 7-m grid (template= the cube, axes=[0, 1]: RA/Dec only, so image the cube on the TP channels).
  3. imsubimage TP, .image and .pb to pb ≥ 0.2, without noisy TP edges.
  4. immath(expr='IM0*IM1'): TP × the interferometer .pb, so both have the same response on the sky.
  5. feather with highres= the .image (not .pbcor) and lowres= TP × pb, then impbcor.

Your choicejoint deconvolution · Rau et al. (2019)

Which method?

Plunkett et al. (2023): SDINT and MACF (model-assisted clean, then feather) give subtly better images. Any combination likely helps with extended emission. Compare with the accuracy parameter (A-par), the power spectrum, source structure and spectral shape.

       outframe='LSRK', cell='0.5arcsec', imsize=800, weighting='briggsbwtaper', robust=0.5,
       pbmask=0.2, restoringbeam='common', niter=10000, threshold='…mJy', usemask='auto-multithresh',
       sidelobethreshold=2.0, noisethreshold=4.25, minbeamfrac=0.3, lownoisethreshold=1.5,
       negativethreshold=0.0, growiterations=75, pbcor=True)
# 2. TP onto the same grid, with the same response on the sky
imhead('tp.image', mode='get', hdkey='restfreq')                       # same as the cube's restfreq?
imregrid(imagename='tp.image', template='int_CO.image', axes=[0, 1], output='tp.regrid')
imsubimage(imagename='tp.regrid', outfile='tp.sub', box='…')            # repeat for .image and .pb
immath(imagename=['tp.sub', 'int_CO.pb.sub'], expr='IM0*IM1', outfile='tp.depb')
# 3. combine, then primary beam correction
feather(imagename='feather.image', highres='int_CO.image.sub', lowres='tp.depb',
        sdfactor=1.0, effdishdiam=-1.0, lowpassfiltersd=False)
impbcor(imagename='feather.image', pbimage='int_CO.pb.sub', outfile='feather.pbcor')

What Total Power measures

  • Each 12-m TP antenna records autocorrelation spectra: the total power in its beam. This covers spacings from 0 (the total flux) to the dish diameter, 12 m.
  • On-the-fly (OTF) maps: the antennas scan the target area in rows, with an emission-free reference position (OFF).
  • No phase, bandpass or flux calibrators. Calibration: a pointing source, Tsys, and Jy/K factors from the observatory database or a jyperk.csv file.
  • TP beam FWHM ≈ 1.13 λ/D before gridding (≈25″ at 230 GHz). Delivered cubes have native channels and 9 cells across the beam.

Comparing combination methods

Aims

  • A uniform, reproducible way to run several combination methods.
  • Which methods give the best image quality?

Method

  • A synthetic continuum sky model: a multiscale molecular cloud with a power-law power spectrum, plus two point sources.
  • The observed M100 CO(1–0) cube (12-m, 7-m and TP), the CASA guide data.

Results

  • SDINT and MACF give subtly better quality than the others.
  • Image quality measured with the accuracy parameter (A-par), Sec. 5.2.
  • With extended emission, any combination helps. Check the spatial power spectrum, source structure and spectral shape.

Checkdiscussion

Four questions

  1. Your C-2 image of a 12″ cloud at Band 6 recovers 15% of its flux. Which tclean parameter fixes this?
    AnswerNone. The cloud is larger than the 12-m MRS (≈10″), so these spacings were not measured. You need 7-m and TP data.
  2. Why not feather TP directly with a 12-m-only image?
    AnswerTP covers spacings up to about 12 m, with little weight beyond ≈8 m. The 12-m array starts at 15 m. The 7-m array fills the gap between them. A larger single dish such as the GBT can overlap the 12-m spacings directly (Hoffman & Kepley 2018).
  3. The feathered image has 20% more total flux than the TP image. What do you check?
    AnswerThe same region in both, TP × pb before feather, the TP flux scale (sdfactor, Jy/K factors), and the rest frequency and channels of both cubes.
  4. Which interferometer image goes into feather, .image or .image.pbcor?
    AnswerThe .image, with the TP image multiplied by the .pb. Run impbcor on the feathered image afterwards.
