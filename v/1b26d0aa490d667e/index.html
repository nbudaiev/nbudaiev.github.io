ALMA data reduction workshop

From visibilities
to science images

Workshop schedule and eight interactive decks for people new to ALMA data. Slides with a CASA call have live controls. Changing a parameter updates the simulated uv-plane, beam, image, spectrum or gain solutions in your browser, on fixed colour scales. Each deck ends with a Backup section of extra slides.

About the simulations

  • 43-antenna layouts matched to ALMA C-2, C-4, C-6 baseline statistics; noise from the radiometer equation.
  • Briggs weighting as CASA defines it; Hogbom and multiscale CLEAN; Kolmogorov phase screens; an antenna-based gain solver.
  • Simplified: Stokes I only, nearest-cell gridding, idealized bandpasses, synthetic listobs and weblog pages. Numbers are illustrative. For proposals use the ALMA Sensitivity Calculator.

Each part is one HTML file plus the shared assets/ folder and runs offline. Follow-along data: the CASA Guides "First Look" TW Hya tutorials (casaguides.nrao.edu), here as Jupyter notebooks for CASA 6.7.4. CASA documentation: casadocs.readthedocs.io.
