Which lines ALMA can see in each band, and what turns a spectrum into a cube.

Conceptwhat lines ALMA sees

Spectral lines in the ALMA bands

TryBand 3 at z = 0 has CO 1–0, the dense-gas lines HCN, HCO⁺, N₂H⁺ 1–0 and the recombination lines H38α–H42α. At z = 4.5 [CII] is in Band 7, at z = 6.5 in Band 6. CO 5–4 (576 GHz) falls in the gap between Bands 8 and 9, where the atmosphere is opaque. The axis is fixed.

FamilyTracesIn the band
MoleculesCold molecular gas: mass (CO), dense gas and chemistry (HCN, HCO⁺, N₂H⁺, CS), motions.
Atoms[CI]: UV-lit cloud surfaces (PDRs). [CII], [NII], [OIII]: distant galaxies, only when redshifted.
Hydrogen HnαIonized gas near young massive stars. With the free-free (Part 4), the electron temperature.

Your choiceuvcontsub · fitspec, fitorder (CASA ≥ 6.5)
Removing the continuum: uvcontsub

  

    

      

      

      
TryStart from the cont.dat ranges. If the fit windows touch the broad line's wings, the continuum is overestimated and the line gets negative dips. One narrow window on one side cannot constrain a slope. fitorder=1 (or 0 with few line-free channels) is usually enough. CASA < 6.5: the old uvcontsub with fitspw (writes <vis>.contsub), now uvcontsub_old.

Set oncerestfreq, veltype
Rest frequency and velocity convention

  

    

      

      

      
TryThe data hold CO 2–1. A rounded rest frequency (230.54, 230.5) shifts the line by 2.6 or 49 km/s, and the cube looks normal. With 13CO the line is near −13,800 km/s. Radio and optical veltype agree at 5 km/s and differ by ≈30 km/s at 3000 km/s (a galaxy). Keep the default 'radio' and say so.

    

    

      
radio: v = c (ν₀ − ν) / ν₀  ·  optical: v = c (ν₀ − ν) / ν  ·  Δv ≈ c Δν / ν

Your choicestart, width, nchan
Choosing the channel width

  

    

      

      

      
TryKeep at least 3 channels across the line FWHM (width ≤ FWHM/3). Wider channels lose the peak and the profile shape. This line is 2.5 native channels wide, so keep the native width. The integrated flux S/N changes little. A width below the native spacing stops tclean with an error ("smaller than smallest original channel width").

    

    
  





  
Your choiceusemask in a cube
Masking options for cubes

  

    

      

      

      
TryLook at Empty mask, per channel. One region leaves ≈ 260 empty beams in every channel, where CLEAN can pick noise peaks. auto-multithresh makes a mask per channel from that channel's noise and follows the line (≈ 5 empty beams, 95% of the line flux). Masks by hand per channel (iclean, no viewer on macOS) work for 15 channels, not for 500. 'pb' masks everything.

    

    

      

        
        
        
      

      
Green: the mask CLEAN may work in. Same colour scale in all three channels (−12 to 68 mJy/beam), as in the channel-map slide.

      

        
Stopping per channel
nsigma=3 stops each channel at 3× its own rms, also the noisier ones near atmospheric lines (Backup: threshold vs nsigma).

        
Absorption
auto-multithresh ignores negative emission when negativethreshold=0 (default). The pipeline uses 15 for cubes (only absorption deeper than 15σ). Lower it (e.g. 5) if you expect real absorption.

      

    

  





  
CheckCARTA · spectral profiler
Channel maps and the spectral profile

  

    

      

      

      
TryStep through the channels. The emission moves from one side of the disk to the other (a "butterfly"). Near the systemic velocity (6 km/s) it lies along the minor axis. At high velocities it is compact, near the star. Click the map for the spectrum of one pixel (in CARTA: the spectral profiler, for a pixel or a region). Your choiceimmoments · moments, includepix, chans
Moment maps and clipping

  

    

      

      

      
TryWithout clipping, moments 1 and 2 are dominated by noise. At 3σ they are less noisy, but moment 0 loses the faint outer disk and its total flux drops. Moment 8 (peak) is robust and good for a first look. Colour scales are fixed, so moment 0 fades as you clip.

Checkcaution
Common issues in line imaging

  

    
Line in the continuum
Lines left inside the continuum selection leak into the continuum image and are oversubtracted from the cube. After uvcontsub, plotms amplitude vs channel should scatter around zero away from the lines.

    
Wrong rest frequency
The cube looks fine but every velocity is shifted. Copy rest frequencies from Splatalogue with all digits. 1 MHz at 230 GHz is 1.3 km/s.

    
Channels narrower than native
No new information and correlated noise. The rms per channel and line S/N are misleading.

    
Per-channel beams
Moments of a cube without a common beam mix resolutions. Use restoringbeam='common' or imsmooth.

    
Jy/beam vs K
Convert with the beam and frequency (imhead, or CARTA). Brightness temperature comparisons need the same beam.

    
Clipped moments
Moment 0 fluxes from clipped maps are biased low. Measure fluxes from the cube, in a mask, or from unclipped moment 0 over the line channels.

  





  
Takeawayreference starting point
Three calls to start a line cube from

  


# 1. continuum subtraction (CASA ≥ 6.5); ranges from the hif_uvcontsub log
uvcontsub(vis='target.ms', outputvis='target_line.ms',
          field='TARGET', spw='25',     # only the spw you image
          fitspec='25:20~505;590~1050;1120~1899', fitorder=1)
# 2. the cube; spw 25: 1920 ch × 0.98 MHz = 1.27 km/s; cell, imsize from Part 4
tclean(vis='target_line.ms', imagename='target_co21',
       field='TARGET', spw='25', specmode='cube',
       restfreq='230.538GHz', outframe='LSRK', veltype='radio',
       start='-63km/s', width='1.3km/s', nchan=104,  # line: −49 to +56 km/s
       gridder='standard', cell='0.06arcsec', imsize=640,
       weighting='briggsbwtaper', robust=0.5,
       niter=100000, nsigma=3.0,    # each channel stops at 3σ
       usemask='auto-multithresh',    # as Part 4, b75 300–400 m:
       sidelobethreshold=2.0, noisethreshold=5.0,
       lownoisethreshold=1.5, minbeamfrac=0.3,
       restoringbeam='common', pbcor=True)
# 3. moment 0 (flux) from .pbcor; clipped moments from .image (flat noise)
immoments(imagename='target_co21.image.pbcor', moments=[0],
          chans='11~92', outfile='target_co21.mom0.pbcor')
immoments(imagename='target_co21.image', moments=[1],
          chans='11~92', includepix=[0.012, 1e9],  # 3σ, σ = 4 mJy/beam
          outfile='target_co21.mom1')

    

      

        
fitspecfrom the hif_uvcontsub log, or cont.dat converted to channels in plotms (LSRK). Check by eye.

        
width≥ native spacing (here 1.27 km/s); ≤ FWHM/3, so ≥ 3 channels across the line

        
start, nchanthe line plus ≥ 10 line-free channels on each side

        
nsigma3; auto-multithresh with the Part 4 values for your b75; negativethreshold=15 (pipeline; e.g. 5 for real absorption)

        
weighting'briggsbwtaper', robust=0.5 (Part 4)

        
moment 0from the pbcor cube, no clip, chans = the line channels

        
moments 1, 2from the .image, includepix=[3σ, 1e9], σ from imstat on line-free channels

        
Done whenrms per channel ≈ expected; line-free channels look like noise

Fixed by the observationspectral setup · channel spacing vs resolution
Channel spacing and spectral resolution

  

    

      

      

      
TryWith no online averaging, Hanning smoothing makes the resolution twice the channel spacing and correlates neighbours. Averaging 2 or 4 channels makes the response close to a box of the new width. This is fixed by the observation setup. In tclean you can only average further (width: the channel-width slide).

    

    

      
      
    

  





  
Fixed by the observationspectral frames · outframe
Doppler setting: TOPO and LSRK

  

    

      

      

      
TryMove execution 2 a few months away from execution 1. ALMA does not Doppler-track. At the start of each execution it tunes so the line falls near the same channel, then keeps the TOPO frequencies fixed. The executions then have different channel frequencies. On one TOPO axis (left) the lines are up to ~45 km/s apart. Regridded to LSRK (right) they line up. tclean does this with outframe='LSRK' (default).

Your choicethreshold, nsigma in cubes
Stopping criteria in cubes

  

    

      

      

      
TrySet nsigma=0 and threshold='9mJy'. Quiet channels stop at 3σ, but the ozone and edge channels are cleaned into the noise. With nsigma=3 every channel stops at 3× its own rms. Then add threshold='15mJy', which sets a floor where it is higher.

    

    

      
      

        
Auto-multithresh in a cube
The mask is built channel by channel, from that channel's noise. For absorption against continuum, auto-multithresh needs negativethreshold > 0 (default 0; pipeline 15; e.g. 5 for real absorption).

        
Big cubes
Image only the channels you need (start, nchan). On a cluster: parallel=True with mpicasa.

Checkevery cube
Cube checklist

  

    
Before imaging
  • Line-free ranges checked by eye: cont.dat against the weblog spectrum, and the subtracted data in plotms.
  • Rest frequency from Splatalogue. width ≥ the native spacing.
  • Expected rms per channel from the weblog, scaled by √(weblog width / your width). Within 1–2 native channels the gain is smaller (correlated channels).

    
After cleaning
  • rms per channel (imstat on line-free channels) close to expected.
  • The first and last channels are line-free (the whole line is in the cube).
  • The mask follows the emission in each channel, and the residual looks like noise.

    
Moments
  • Clip on the .image (flat noise), not on .image.pbcor.
  • Fluxes from the pbcor cube in a mask, or from an unclipped moment 0 of the pbcor cube over the line channels.
  • Write down the clip level and the channel range you used.

    
Keep
  • The uvcontsub, tclean and immoments calls in a script.
  • exportfits the cube, pbcor cube and moments you publish.
  • Report restfreq, frame, veltype and width with your results.

  





  
Checkquestions
Four questions before the hands-on session

  
    
    
  1. Your spw has 3840 channels of 122 kHz at 230 GHz. What is the channel spacing in km/s, and roughly the spectral resolution?
    AnswerΔv = c·Δν/ν ≈ 3×10⁵ × 0.122/230000 ≈ 0.16 km/s. With Hanning smoothing and no online averaging the resolution is ≈2× that, 0.32 km/s. So width='0.16km/s' is the finest grid tclean accepts.
    2. 
    
  2. The weblog's mom8_fc preview of your spw 25 cube shows the disk. What went wrong, and what do you change?
    AnswerLine emission is in channels that cont.dat calls line-free. It was fitted as continuum, so the continuum image is contaminated and the cube lost flux. Cut those channels out of fitspec, rerun uvcontsub, and reimage both.
    3. 
    
  3. Your continuum-subtracted cube shows negative dips on both sides of a broad line. Cause?
    AnswerThe fitspec ranges included the line wings, so the continuum was overestimated there. Widen the excluded range around the line.
    4. 
    
  4. Moment 0 with includepix=[3σ, 1e9] gives 20% less flux than the cube spectrum. Which is right?
    AnswerThe cube spectrum (in a mask or aperture). Clipping removes the faint emission below 3σ in each channel, and that adds up. Make moment 0 without clipping, over the line channels only.
    5.
