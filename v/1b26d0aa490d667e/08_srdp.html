Restore

Raw data ⊗ calibration tables. Reproduces the calibration that was checked at QA2. scriptForPI.py calls hifa_restoredata (no need to run it on its own).

Pipeline run

Calculates the calibration tables again. If you run it yourself, you can change how they are calculated.

  • use a newer pipeline version
  • add or remove an execution (ASDM), change the reference antenna

Checkhifa_renorm · data up to Cycle 7

Amplitude renormalization

TryTsys is measured off source. Line emission that is bright in the target's autocorrelations lowers the calibrated amplitudes in those channels. At 10 K in Band 6 the scaling reaches 1.10 and the line peak is 9% low. Below 1.02 no correction is applied. createcaltable=False only assesses.

  • Affected: FDM spws with strong lines, bright in a single-dish spectrum of the target (e.g. Galactic molecular clouds, hot cores). TDM spws need no renormalization.
  • The scaling spectrum is computed per spw, scan, field, antenna and polarization, If its maximum exceeds threshold, the whole spectrum is applied.
  • Check the QA2 report or the hifa_renorm weblog page. Correcting older data: Backup.

Checkhifa_imageprecheck · the robust value

Requested resolution and robust

TryThe pipeline predicts the beam for robust 0, 0.5, 1 and 2 (0.5 only for the 7-m array). It takes the value closest to 0.5 whose beam area is in the requested range (the science goal's, or the AUDI form's Angular Resolution). If none is in range, it takes 2 when all beams are too small and 0 when too large, QA score 0.25. AUDI may also add a uvtaper. To use your own robust, skip this stage.

Checkhif_checkproductsize · size mitigation

Product size mitigation

TryWith 0.1″ and 3840 channels the cube is above 40 GB, so its channels are binned by 2. At 0.04″ the field shrinks too. With 3840 channels, Band 3 at 0.04″ stays above 60 GB and cannot be mitigated.

Sketch of the cascade (Hunter et al. 2023), 4 identical spws, single fields. Size = 4 bytes × nx × ny × nchan / nbin, ×2 for the PB. Full size: mitigate=False or the tclean calls in casa_commands.log.

Your choicecasa_pipescript.py · imaging stages

Editing casa_pipescript.py for imaging

import glob; MyVis = glob.glob('*.ms')   # the calibrated MSs here
context = h_init()
try:
    hifa_importdata(vis=MyVis, dbservice=False)
    hif_mstransform()                  # writes *_targets.ms
    hifa_flagtargets()                 # reads uid*flagtargetstemplate.txt
    # hifa_imageprecheck()             # picks robust, skipped: robust=1.5 below
    hif_checkproductsize(maxproductsize=350.0, maxcubesize=40.0,
                         maxcubelimit=60.0)
    hif_makeimlist(specmode='mfs')     # per-spw continuum
    hif_findcont()                     # keeps ranges in cont.dat, finds the rest
    hif_uvcontsub()                    # uses cont.dat
    hif_makeimages()
    hif_makeimlist(specmode='cont')    # aggregate continuum
    hif_makeimages()
    hif_makeimlist(specmode='cube', spw='17,23', nbins='17:8,23:2', robust=1.5)
    hif_makeimages()
    hifa_exportdata()
finally:
    h_save()

All cases: CASA guide ALMA Imaging Pipeline Reprocessing. Parameters: Pipeline Reference Manual.

Notes

  • hif_makeimages always follows a hif_makeimlist.
  • Own robust: set it in hif_makeimlist, skip hifa_imageprecheck.
  • No mitigation: skip hif_checkproductsize, or set nbins, hm_imsize, hm_cell.
  • Directory: the calibrated MSs, cont.dat, uid*flagtargetstemplate.txt. Delete old uid*_targets.ms first.

Example cases

  • Aggregate continuum with all channels: rename cont.dat, skip hif_findcont and hif_uvcontsub.
  • Revised continuum ranges: edit cont.dat (Part 5).
  • Cubes for some spws or fields, with binning, robust or uvtaper (highlighted line).

Automated self-calibration

hif_selfcal in the ALMA and VLA pipelines and in AUDI, and the standalone auto_selfcal tool.

Conceptauto_selfcal · hif_selfcal

Auto-selfcal: how it works

Runs in AUDI (Attempt self-calibration), the ALMA and VLA pipelines (hif_selfcal), and the standalone tool github.com/jjtobin/auto_selfcal (input: the pipeline *_targets.ms). Weblog page: Part 6.

Pass if

  • S/N and near-field S/N do not drop (rms outside the mask). inf_EB may lose up to 2% S/N.
  • beam area up by less than 5%, rms up by less than 5%
  • near-field: outside the mask plus a buffer, where self-cal changes the noise most

Output

  • applied: the last successful table (plus inf_EB), in CORRECTED_DATA of *_targets.ms
  • continuum and cube images with and without self-cal, both in the archive

Your choicehif_selfcal · dividing_factor

Large phase errors: dividing_factor

TryThe first clean stops at peak / dividing_factor, but not below 5 × rms. At the default 15 the floor sets it, deep enough to put artifacts into the model (about half its flux outside the circle). Self-cal fits the data to this model and part of the pattern stays. At 1.5 only the peak is cleaned, and one round removes the phase errors.

A 20 mJy point source, C-4-like array, Band 6, 1 h, constant antenna phase offsets of 60° rms. Model: clean components ⊛ beam. Phase-only, one solution per antenna for all data (inf_EB).

Checkauto-selfcal · long baselines

Long baselines: flagged outer antennas

TryA resolved source has less flux on long baselines, so the outer antennas give low-S/N solutions that gaincal flags. Auto-selfcal smooths the flagged fraction vs distance and finds where it starts to rise (rate of change and acceleration). If the flags make the beam grow by more than 5%, antennas beyond that point flagged at least as often as the smoothed curve there get gains of 1+0j (no correction), so their data are kept. The short baselines still improve. Raise the flux and the point moves out.

Sketch of the heuristic: C-6-like array, Band 6, a 0.25″ Gaussian, eight 60-s solutions per antenna with all spws.

When to go manual

Line self-cal, polarized targets, ephemeris targets, faint mosaic fields (Part 6). Manual self-cal is rarely much better otherwise.

Checkhifa_renorm · details

Renormalization: details

Cause

  • The correlator normalizes the cross-correlations by the autocorrelations.
  • Tsys is measured at a reference position off source (typically 2′ away).
  • Where the target's line emission adds power to the autocorrelations, the calibrated amplitudes are low by about Tline / Tsys.

How hifa_renorm works

  • Target autocorrelations ÷ bandpass calibrator autocorrelations (assumed line-free).
  • Polynomial baseline up to 5th order, for differences in airmass and time.
  • Scaling spectrum per spw, scan, field, antenna and polarization, applied above threshold=1.02.
  • FDM spws only. Bands 9 and 10 rarely need it.

Older data

  • Check the QA2 report or weblog: was renormalization checked?
  • If not, run it on the restored data in calibrated/working/ with the latest pipeline release (CASA 6.6.6 or later), then applycal its table.
  • Current CASA writes a calibration table, applied with applycal.
  • CASA guide ALMA Renormalization Correction, ALMA knowledgebase article 623.

Checkdiscussion

Four questions

  1. Your Cycle 6 Band 6 cube of a hot core shows strong CH₃OH lines. The QA2 report does not mention renormalization. What do you do?
    AnswerRun the renormalization assessment on the restored data. Lines that are bright in the autocorrelations may be several percent too faint. If the scaling exceeds 1.02, apply the correction table before imaging.
  2. The weblog cube has channels twice as wide as the spw's channels, and the field is smaller than the primary beam. Why?
    Answerhif_checkproductsize mitigated a cube above 40 GB: steps 1a (nbin=2) and 1b (PB level above 0.2). For full products use AUDI, scriptForReprocessing.py with mitigate=False, or the tclean call in casa_commands.log.
  3. hifa_imageprecheck chose robust = 2.0 with QA score 0.25. What does that tell you?
    AnswerNo robust value gave a beam in the requested range, and all predicted beams were too small. The data have higher resolution than requested. A uvtaper can bring the beam into the range (AUDI may compute one, or use uvtaper in your own tclean).
  4. Auto-selfcal reports success, but spokes around the bright source do not go away. What could cause it?
    AnswerLarge phase errors put artifacts into the first model, and self-cal then fitted the data to them. Rerun from DATA with a lower dividing_factor (e.g. 3) in hif_selfcal or the standalone tool.
